Last updated: 2026-07-22 · Recall ID: 82393

Product

Certain Social Sessions edible cannabis products

Issue

What you should do

Stop using the products if they are part of the recallReturn the products to the store where you bought them or dispose of them. Dispose of any products that you have opened safely and out of the reach of children and youth.Report any health or safety complaints you have about these or other cannabis products by filling out the Cannabis Reporting Form online.
