Boon PIVOT Toddler Tower Kids Kitchen Step Stool recalled due to tip-over hazard

Last updated: 2026-07-16 · Recall ID: 82323

Product

Boon PIVOT Toddler Tower Kids Kitchen Step Stool

Issue

Tip-over hazard

What you should do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact TOMY International, Inc. for a repair kit.For more information, consumers can contact TOMY Consumer Care Team by telephone at 1-866-725-4407 between 8:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. CT Monday through Friday, by email at cs@tomy.com or visit the company website.Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and TOMY International, Inc. Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.
