Health Canada warns of very rare but serious risk of vision loss with semaglutide drugs

Last updated: 2026-11-25 · Recall ID: 83010

Product

Prescription semaglutide drugs

Issue

New safety information

What you should do

Discuss the potential risks of using any health product with your healthcare provider before you start taking a product.If you experience sudden vision loss or rapidly worsening eyesight while taking semaglutide (e.g., blurring or cloudiness of vision), seek urgent medical attention. Your doctor may refer you to an eye specialist (ophthalmologist) for an examination. If NAION is confirmed, you may need to stop taking the medication.If you have questions or concerns about any health product you are taking, speak with a health care professional such as your doctor or pharmacist.You can report any health product-related side effects or complaints (e.g. quality concerns) to Health Canada.Information for healthcare professionals:There is no identified time interval for when NAION may develop after starting treatment.Patients reporting a sudden vision loss, including partial vision loss, should be urgently referred for ophthalmological examination.Treatment with semaglutide should be discontinued if NAION is confirmed.Related links:Summary Safety Review - Semaglutide - Assessing the Potential Risk of Non-arteritic Anterior Ischemic Optic Neuropathy (2026-11-25)
