Trek and Electra brand bicycles with coaster brakes recalled due to fall hazard

Last updated: 2025-11-13 · Recall ID: 78485

Product

Trek and Electra brand bicycles

Issue

Fall hazard

What you should do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled products and contact their local authorized Trek retailer for free replacement of the back wheel. For more information, consumers can contact the Trek Customer Care Team by telephone at 1-800-373-4594, Monday through Friday, from 8 am to 6 pm CT, by email at customercare@trekbikes.com, or visit the company website.Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and Trek Bicycle Corporation.Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.
