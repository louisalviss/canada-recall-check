​​Certain 3-inch Grundfos SQFlex Solar Submersible Pumps​ recalled due to electric shock hazard

Last updated: 2026-09-29 · Recall ID: 82678

Product

Grundfos 3-inch SQFlex Solar Submersible Pumps

Issue

Electrical hazard

What you should do

Consumers should immediately stop using affected pumps installed on the surface, such as those used in tanks and ponds, and contact Grundfos or your installer for a repair or replacement. Affected pumps that have not been installed yet should be returned for a replacement. For pumps installed in wells, no immediate action is required, but affected pumps should be identified and isolated and Grundfos contacted when the pump is removed from the well for any reason in the future.For more information, consumers can contact Grundfos Canada Inc. by telephone at 1-844-994-2850, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., CST, by email at recall@grundfos.com or visit the company's website. Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.
