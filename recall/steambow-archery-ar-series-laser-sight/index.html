​​Steambow Archery AR-Series Laser Sight (Green)​ recalled due to eye injury hazard

Last updated: 2026-06-05 · Recall ID: 82150

Product

Steambow Archery AR-Series Laser Sight

Issue

Injury hazard

What you should do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and return it to Leapfrog Outdoor Sports and Apparel for a refund.For more information, consumers can contact Leapfrog Outdoor Sports and Apparel by telephone at 587-486-0563, Tuesday to Friday from 10:00-17:00 MST, by email at info@leapfrogoutdoor.ca or visit the company website.Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.
