Cambridge Audio Yoyo (M) Portable Bluetooth Speakers recalled due to fire hazard

Last updated: 2026-09-22 · Recall ID: 82647

Product

Cambridge Audio Yoyo (M) Portable Bluetooth Speakers

Issue

Fire hazard

What you should do

Consumers who have these affected products should immediately stop using them and contact Cambridge Audio to verify if the product is affected by the recall.Consumers should keep the recalled speakers until they receive further instruction from Cambridge Audio. Keep the speakers switched off and unplugged and do not charge them. Store them somewhere cool, dry and out of the way, clear of anything that burns easily (such as paper, cardboard and flammable liquids), and away from radiators, direct sunlight and excessive heat. The refund process can be found at the company's website. Once Cambridge Audio has processed the claim and confirmed a refund, the consumer may dispose of the product safely in such a way that they cannot be used again. Contact your municipality for instructions on how to safely transport and dispose of lithium-ion batteries. Batteries are considered hazardous waste. Do not place them in household garbage.For more information, consumers can contact Cambridge Audio/Audio Partnership Plc by telephone at 888-805-5001, from 9am to 5pm Eastern Time (ET), Monday to Friday, by email at recall.canada@cambridgeaudio.com or visit the company website.Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.
