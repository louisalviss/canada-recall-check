Finnleo, Tylo, Polar, Radiant Health, and Helo Brand Saunas and Infrared Kits recalled due to fire and burn hazards

Last updated: 2026-09-17 · Recall ID: 82120

Product

Finnleo, Tylo, Polar, Radiant Health, and Helo Brand Saunas and Infrared Kits

Issue

Fire hazard - Burn hazard

What you should do

Consumers should immediately stop using the recalled product and contact Sauna360 to schedule a free in-home repair from a professional installer.For more information, consumers can contact Sauna360 by telephone at 1-866-936-5962, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET, Monday to Friday or by email at recall@sauna360.com.Joint recall with Health Canada, the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission (US CPSC) and Sauna360.Please note that the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act prohibits recalled products from being redistributed, sold or even given away in Canada.Health Canada would like to remind consumers to report any health or safety incidents related to the use of this product or any other consumer product or cosmetic by filling out the Consumer Product Incident Report Form.This recall is also posted on the OECD Global Portal on Product Recalls website. You can visit this site for more information on other international consumer product recalls.
