Health Canada warns of inaccurate radon detectors sold online

Last updated: 2026-11-16 · Recall ID: 82955

Product

Radon testing devices, including electronic radon monitors and detector kits used to measure radon levels in homes.

Issue

Impact hazard - Injury hazard

What you should do

Test your home for radon. The best time to test is during winter, when homes are more tightly sealed and radon levels are often higher.Use an approved long-term radon test lasting at least three months. This will provide the most accurate assessment of your home's average radon level.Only purchase electronic radon monitors that have passed performance testing by the Canadian National Radon Proficiency Program (C-NRPP), the Canadian radon certification program recognized by Health Canada.If you have purchased a recalled electronic radon detector, stop using it and dispose of it according to municipal electronic waste requirements.Report any incidents involving a consumer product to Health Canada: Consumer Product Incident Report Form.Related links:Radon - Canada.caTake Action on radonGuide for radon measurements in homes - Canada.ca
