Health Canada warns about unauthorized peptides, including contaminated injectable peptides sold by Sage & Summit Wellness Co. in Ottawa, ON

Last updated: 2026-11-12 · Recall ID: 82944

Product

Unauthorized peptides (injectable and capsule format)

Issue

Unauthorized product - Contamination

What you should do

If you have used an unauthorized peptide product and experience adverse effects or feel unwell, seek medical attention. If you have injected it and you develop redness, swelling, pain at the injection site, fever, or chills, seek immediate medical attention, as these may be signs of a serious infection.Do not buy or use unauthorized health products. Authorized health products have an eight-digit Drug Identification Number (DIN) or Natural Product Number (NPN) on the label. You can also check whether a drug has been authorized for sale by searching Health Canada's Drug Product Database or Licensed Natural Health Products Database.Do not use drugs that are labelled with any variation of “For Research Use Only”. These are intended for lab use, not human consumption, and can be dangerous to your health.Use prescription drugs under the care of a healthcare professional and only buy prescription drugs from licensed pharmacies. Be aware of the risks of buying health products online.Dispose of unauthorized peptide products by following municipal or regional guidance for chemicals and other hazardous waste or take the product to your local pharmacy for proper disposal.Report any health product-related side effects or complaints to Health Canada. Your reports help identify potentially dangerous products and protect others from harm.Visit Health Canada’s webpage on Peptides to learn more about peptides, their health risks, and steps you can take to protect yourself.Related links:Health Canada peptide safety advisoriesHealth Canada information on peptides
