Safety Recall - Transport Canada is warning of a risk of capsize on Sea-Doo Switch 2022 to 2026 models

Last updated: 2026-01-05 · Recall ID: 78409

Product

Sea-Doo Switch

Issue

Risk of capsize

What you should do

Contact your dealer so that they can complete the recall. If you choose to use your Sea-Doo Switch before the recall repair is made, you need to know the boat may be more sensitive to front overload as the water may not drain as intended under specific conditions.Bombardier Recreational Products (BRP) recommends the following additional safety measures:Before every ride, everyone onboard should:Read and understand the instructions on the correct distribution of passenger and cargo weight to prevent front overloading;Understand the new warning label; andWatch the safety videoReplaces bulletins 2025-4 and 2025-10 Transport Canada web safety recall notice.
